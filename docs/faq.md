# Frequently asked questions

## What kinds of projects are a good fit?

API and developer documentation, product onboarding, setup and troubleshooting guides, AI/RAG product documentation, operational procedures, and focused content reviews are possible project types. The [Field Guide](field-guide/index.md) maps the wider discipline, but a topic appearing there is not a claim of prior WritersAdda delivery experience. We scope work against relevant experience, access, and the review expertise available.

## Which industries do you work in?

The field guide covers software and IT, industrial products, transportation, healthcare, finance, infrastructure, telecommunications, and other contexts. The public portfolio currently demonstrates software, APIs, and AI/RAG. Other sectors are considered case by case; regulated and safety-critical content requires qualified client-side domain reviewers and explicit approval controls.

## Can you work with our tools or standards?

The [tool map](field-guide/tools.md) includes representative authoring, publishing, wiki, API, structured-content, and diagramming platforms. It is a field reference, not a list of tools WritersAdda claims to have used. We confirm platform-specific experience and workflow fit during scoping. The same applies to standards and regulatory requirements.

## Can WritersAdda guarantee regulatory compliance?

No. Clear documentation can support a compliance process, but it cannot establish compliance by itself. Your qualified regulatory, legal, quality, safety, and engineering owners must identify requirements and approve controlled content.

## Can we start with one guide or a small audit?

Yes. A focused deliverable is often a useful way to understand the product, review process, and collaboration before expanding the scope. We agree on goals, inputs, reviewers, and delivery format before work begins.

## Do you work with our engineers and product experts?

Yes. Subject-matter experts help confirm product behavior and resolve open questions. WritersAdda organizes the content, drafts it for readers, and coordinates review against the agreed scope.

## Can you work in our docs-as-code repository?

Potentially. Markdown, pull requests, and Git-based review can be part of the engagement when they fit your tools and access requirements. We confirm repository access and workflow during scoping.

## How do you handle confidential product information?

Access, confidentiality expectations, and any required agreement should be discussed before sharing sensitive material. Do not send credentials or confidential data through an unsecured channel.

## Do you publish client work in the portfolio?

Only with the client's permission. Current portfolio samples are self-directed demonstrations, not client deliverables. Any future case study should clearly state the work performed and use evidence the client approves.

## What should I include in an inquiry?

A short description of the product, audience, documentation need, current challenge, and target timeline is enough to start. You do not need a finished brief.
