# About WritersAdda

WritersAdda is a founder-led technical documentation studio for teams building software, APIs, and AI-enabled products. We make specialist product knowledge easier to understand, use, and keep current.

The studio is built on **10 years of technical writing experience**. That experience informs a practical approach: learn the product, understand what readers need to do, and work closely with engineers and product experts to publish accurate content.

## How we think about documentation

- **Start with the reader's task.** Organize information around what people need to accomplish.
- **Stay close to the product.** Use product access, code, and subject-matter reviews to ground the writing.
- **Make examples useful.** Show realistic paths and call out assumptions or limitations.
- **Plan for change.** Prefer maintainable source content and clear ownership over one-off pages that quickly drift.
- **Be clear about evidence.** Distinguish verified product behavior from illustrative examples.

## Areas of focus

API and developer documentation, product onboarding, setup and troubleshooting, AI/RAG product guides, and documentation audits.
