# Technical documentation services

Technical content support for product teams and organizations that need complex information to be accurate, usable, and maintainable. Current published samples focus on software, APIs, and AI/RAG; work in other domains is scoped against relevant experience, product access, and qualified subject-matter review.

## Start with a focused review

If you are not sure what to commission, begin with a **Documentation Clarity Review** for one key user journey. We review the relevant content and, where access permits, follow the documented task. You receive prioritized observations, open questions for your product team, and practical options for next steps. The scope and fee are agreed before the review starts.

This is a proposed starting engagement, not a fixed-price package. [Discuss a review](contact.md).

## API and developer documentation

Help developers understand your product and integrate with confidence.

- API references and endpoint guides
- Authentication, errors, and request/response examples
- SDK and integration guides
- Developer quickstarts and tutorials
- OpenAPI review and documentation structure

## Product and user documentation

Help users, administrators, installers, and service teams complete product tasks and understand technical concepts.

- Installation, configuration, operation, maintenance, and upgrade guides
- User, administrator, installer, and service documentation
- Quick-start guides, knowledge-base content, and troubleshooting
- AI/ML and retrieval-augmented generation concepts and workflows
- Technical product overviews and architecture explanations

## Operational documentation and content systems

Help teams capture repeatable work and improve how documentation is organized, reviewed, and maintained.

- SOPs, work instructions, checklists, and operational runbooks
- Content and information architecture reviews
- Docs-as-code setup and Markdown migration
- Structured authoring and content reuse planning
- Style, terminology, and consistency reviews
- Documentation backlog assessment and prioritization
- Editorial, SME-review, and publishing workflows

## Technical marketing and decision support

Support subject-matter experts with clear, technically grounded content for product evaluation and business decisions.

- Technical white papers, solution briefs, and product explainers
- RFP/RFI response sections and technical questionnaires
- Case-study content based on client-approved evidence and attribution
- Technical sections of proposals and product requirement materials

Product claims, roadmap statements, customer outcomes, and business metrics must be verified and approved by the responsible client stakeholders.

## How engagements work

We agree on the audience, scope, access, reviewers, deliverables, review rounds, and completion criteria before writing begins. Work can be scoped as an audit, a single guide, or a small documentation sprint. Availability and timelines are confirmed during the initial conversation.

## What we need from you

Useful inputs include access to the product or a demo, existing content, technical contacts for review, a clear view of the intended audience, and any applicable approval requirements. For regulated or safety-critical material, the client's qualified domain and compliance owners remain accountable for technical and regulatory approval. If inputs are not ready, we can identify a discovery step first.
