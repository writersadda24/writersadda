# Frequently asked questions

## What kinds of projects are a good fit?

API and developer documentation, product onboarding, setup and troubleshooting guides, AI/RAG product documentation, and focused audits are a good fit for WritersAdda. If your need is outside these areas, share the context and we can discuss fit during an initial conversation.

## Can we start with one guide or a small audit?

Yes. A focused deliverable is often a useful way to understand the product, review process, and collaboration before expanding the scope. We agree on goals, inputs, reviewers, and delivery format before work begins.

## Do you work with our engineers and product experts?

Yes. Subject-matter experts help confirm product behavior and resolve open questions. WritersAdda organizes the content, drafts it for readers, and coordinates review against the agreed scope.

## Can you work in our docs-as-code repository?

Potentially. Markdown, pull requests, and Git-based review can be part of the engagement when they fit your tools and access requirements. We confirm repository access and workflow during scoping.

## How do you handle confidential product information?

Access, confidentiality expectations, and any required agreement should be discussed before sharing sensitive material. Do not send credentials or confidential data through an unsecured channel.

## Do you publish client work in the portfolio?

Only with the client's permission. Current portfolio samples are self-directed demonstrations, not client deliverables. Any future case study should clearly state the work performed and use evidence the client approves.

## What should I include in an inquiry?

A short description of the product, audience, documentation need, current challenge, and target timeline is enough to start. You do not need a finished brief.
