# About WritersAdda

WritersAdda is a founder-led technical documentation studio for teams building software, APIs, and AI-enabled products. We make specialist product knowledge easier to understand, use, and keep current.

The studio is built on **10 years of technical writing experience**. As a founder-led practice, WritersAdda gives clients a direct working relationship with the technical writer from scoping through handoff. The work starts with learning the product, understanding what readers need to do, and collaborating with engineers and product experts to publish accurate content.

## How we think about documentation

- **Start with the reader's task.** Organize information around what people need to accomplish.
- **Stay close to the product.** Use product access, code, and subject-matter reviews to ground the writing.
- **Make examples useful.** Show realistic paths and call out assumptions or limitations.
- **Plan for change.** Prefer maintainable source content and clear ownership over one-off pages that quickly drift.
- **Be clear about evidence.** Distinguish verified product behavior from illustrative examples.

## Areas of focus

Current published work demonstrates API and developer documentation, software setup, and AI/RAG product guidance. WritersAdda also provides a broader field guide to documentation types, industries, tools, and workflows. That educational coverage should not be confused with a claim of hands-on experience in every area; new work is scoped against relevant experience and available subject-matter review.
